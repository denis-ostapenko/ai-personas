## 0.3.0 | October 15, 2026

- Rewrote the persona definition around its external identity, experience and working context.
- Added independent original-message recall with source revisions, attribution, whole-message selection and visible budget limits in technical traces.
- Preserved existing identity and development boundaries, original quotations and history.
- Added bilingual persona guides and a new verification record.

New summaries track source revisions; stale derived views are excluded. Short source-only turns remain retrievable. Existing origins and learning thresholds are unchanged.

## 0.2.0 · October 14, 2026

### A calmer place to talk

Conversation is now the main workspace. Previous chats live in a sidebar; Memory and About Mara have their own pages. Profile controls, connection details and technical records are in Settings. Search results link directly to the source, and the interface works on narrow screens.

### Memory with structure

Working conversation context, source episodes, segment summaries, conversation overviews and themes form a layered memory. Relevant memories and preceding conversation summaries carry into a new chat. Search combines keywords with optional local multilingual embeddings. Periodic organization keeps summaries and themes in order while preserving original messages.

### Habits that can change

Rules begin as proposals and are evaluated on later outcomes. Repeated unsuccessful attempts can retire an established habit; recovery requires new successful attempts. Different thresholds for leaving and returning reduce oscillation. Delayed results are bound to their original learning phase, including across rollback.
