# Persona: release notes

## 0.5.0 | October 15, 2026

- Rewrote the persona definition around its external identity, experience and working context.
- Added independent original-message recall with source revisions, attribution, whole-message selection and visible budget limits in technical traces.
- Preserved existing identity and development boundaries, original quotations and history.
- Added bilingual persona guides and a new verification record.

## 0.4.2 | October 14, 2026

- Added explicit English and Brazilian Portuguese conversation rules, including language switching without resetting the character or memory.
- Existing chats receive the language update while retaining their own character settings.
- New internal topic titles are authored in English; saved quotations retain their original wording.
- Added bilingual descriptions, strengths, weaknesses and release plans.
- Adopted MIT, copyright Denis Ostapenko.

### 0.4.1 | October 14, 2026

- Separated general emotional reactions from changes in trust and affinity toward the user.
- Limited interpersonal conflict and apology repair to actions involving the persona.
- Recomputed remaining friction after forgetting related events.
- Added environment-based startup and complete backup/restore.

### Earlier releases

0.4.0 introduced the authored temperament model, six controls, mood, relationship state and event replay. 0.3.0 added SQLite memory, reaction cells and correction history. 0.2.0 separated identity, memory and conversation. 0.1 introduced the local application and memory editing.
