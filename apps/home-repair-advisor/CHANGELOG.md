# Home Repair Advisor: release notes

## 0.4.0 | October 15, 2026

- Rewrote the persona definition around its external identity, experience and working context.
- Added independent original-message recall with source revisions, attribution, whole-message selection and visible budget limits in technical traces.
- Preserved existing identity and development boundaries, original quotations and history.
- Added bilingual persona guides and a new verification record.

## 0.3.2 | October 14, 2026

- Replaced the English-only response rule with English and Brazilian Portuguese conversation support.
- Extended the customer's language to generated assessments, questions, job briefs and provider explanations.
- Kept US search phrases, structured identifiers and original evidence quotations unchanged in language.
- Preserved existing cases through the language-only identity revision.
- Added bilingual descriptions and release plans; adopted MIT, copyright Denis Ostapenko.

### 0.3.1 | October 14, 2026

- Added environment-based startup, complete backup/restore and a standalone repository package.

### Earlier releases

0.3.0 introduced quoted case facts, a correction journal and separate job epochs. 0.2 added numeric budgets, business review profiles and an optional Google Places adapter. 0.1 introduced photo assessment, ZIP lookup, local searches and saved cases.
